# Image, icon and font credits

Every graphic file served by Framapetitions, with its author, origin and
licence. Keep this list up to date when adding a file: only add files
whose licence is free and stated by the author or the publisher.
A short version is shown to visitors at the bottom of every page
(templates/layouts/footer.html).

## Icons

- Author: Fonticons, Inc., https://fontawesome.com
- Licence: icons under CC BY 4.0, https://fontawesome.com/license/free
